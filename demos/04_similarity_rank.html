Query + Search • Two steps on one page

Your question becomes a fingerprint — then we rank

This page shows two pipeline steps: Query (question → numbers) and Search (rank chunks). Use the pipeline bar above to switch focus.

Query — your question as numbers

The question goes through the same Encoder as the PDF slices — that’s why “hospitals” can match “Medical Center”. No fingerprint, no search.

Search — rank chunks by meaning
Threshold > 0.30

Top-K chunks are the only ones sent to the LLM. Others are ignored — like discarding 40 pages of legal fluff.

For Builders — cosine

cosine = dot(a,b) for normalized vectors. Here Top-K=4. Real DBs use ANN for 10k+ vectors.
