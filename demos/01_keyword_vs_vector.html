[CMP] Keyword vs Vector

Keyword misses, meaning finds

Policy sentence: “Covered providers: City General and St. Jude’s Medical Center.” You search for “Hospitals”.

How it works

  1. Keyword match: same letters only — “Hospitals” never hits “Medical Center”.
  2. Vector match: same meaning, even with different letters.
  3. That’s why RAG Search uses fingerprints, not Ctrl+F.
